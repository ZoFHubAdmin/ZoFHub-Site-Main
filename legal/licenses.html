ZoFHub's web-browser feature is built on open-source software that is served to your browser when you use it. Several of those components are copyleft (AGPL-3.0 / LGPL-3.0), which means anyone who uses them over a network is entitled to their source code. This page is that offer, and it links the verbatim license text for each component.
