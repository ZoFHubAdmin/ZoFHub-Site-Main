Note for game owners: ZoFHub does not host third-party games; we embed or link to them. The fastest fix is often to request removal from the original host, but we will promptly remove or disable any embed/link on the Site upon a valid notice.

1. Our role, and the safe harbours we rely on

ZoFHub is an intermediary, not a publisher. It performs two functions, and the limitations on liability that apply to each are different.

  • The game catalogue lists games that run from third-party servers inside an embedded frame. ZoFHub does not host, store, mirror, re-upload or modify any game binary or asset; it stores catalogue metadata and an embed address, and it links to material held by others. We operate it as an information location tool within the meaning of 17 U.S.C. § 512(d).
  • The browser and proxy features transmit material between a user and a destination that user chooses. ZoFHub does not select the material or its recipients, does not initiate the transmission, and does not modify the content; storage is automatic, transient and no longer than is needed to carry the transmission. We operate them as transitory digital network communications within the meaning of 17 U.S.C. § 512(a).

We rely, in the same way, on the equivalent intermediary provisions of other jurisdictions — including Articles 12–14 of the EU e-Commerce Directive and Article 4 of the EU Digital Services Act.

These protections are earned by conduct, not claimed by statement. What we actually do is set out in the rest of this policy: a designated agent to receive notices, an expeditious response to valid ones, a counter-notification route for material removed in error, and termination of repeat infringers. If you are a rights-holder and a listing on this Site points to your work without permission, tell us and we will remove it. You do not need to prove anything about the third-party host first, and we will not ask you to take it up with them instead.

2. Filing a copyright infringement notice

To file a notice of claimed infringement, please provide a written communication (by email to the contact below) that includes all of the following:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature;
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed (or a representative list if multiple works);
  3. Identification of the material you claim is infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it — please include the exact URL(s) on the Site;
  4. Your contact information (name, address, telephone number, and email);
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

If you believe your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notification to the contact above that includes:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature;
  2. Identification of the material that was removed and the location where it appeared before removal;
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification;
  4. Your name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate court and will accept service of process from the party who filed the original notice.

If we receive a valid counter-notification, we may restore the material unless the original complainant files a court action within the period allowed by law.

We will, in appropriate circumstances, disable or terminate access for users who are repeat infringers.
