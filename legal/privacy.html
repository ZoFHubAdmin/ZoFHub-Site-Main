Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZoFHub ("ZoFHub", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit {{SITE_URL}} (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Community posts & recommendations — any text you choose to post.
  • Feedback — if you use "Send feedback", we store your message, the page you sent it from, an irreversible hash of your IP address, your browser's user-agent string, and an email address only if you choose to enter one. Feedback is private: it is never shown publicly and is readable only by the site operator.
  • AI prompts — we do not receive them. The AI assistant runs in your browser and contacts the AI provider directly from your own device, so your prompts, the replies, and any generated images never pass through our servers and are never logged or stored by us (see Section 5).

Information collected automatically

  • Device & usage data — browser type, device type, pages viewed, and general interaction data used to keep the Site working and improve it.
  • Server logs — our host records standard log data, which may include your IP address, for security, abuse-prevention, and reliability.
  • Cookies & local storage — small files and browser storage used for preferences, your generated display name, favorites, and advertising. See our Cookie Policy.

We do not ask for your real name, email, password, or payment details to play. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the Site (load games, serve the community wall, and deliver the AI assistant that then runs in your browser).
  • To remember your preferences, favorites, and display name on your device.
  • To protect the Site against abuse, fraud, and technical problems.
  • To measure and improve performance and content.
  • To display advertising on supported pages.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser local storage for essential functions and to remember your settings. We set no advertising cookies of our own. The third-party ad networks named in Section 4 may set their own cookies and similar identifiers when their ads load — those belong to them, not to us, and we cannot read them. You can control or delete cookies in your browser at any time. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

The advertising frequency cap

If a full-screen ad is shown between games, we store one timestamp in your browser's local storage recording when it was last shown, so that it cannot be shown to you again for at least twenty minutes. That timestamp is the whole record: it is not an identifier, it contains nothing about you or the game you opened, it is never sent to our servers or to an ad network, and it cannot be read by any other site. If local storage is unavailable — private browsing, or storage switched off — we cannot count, so no full-screen ad is shown at all. Clearing your browser data removes it.

The ad-block check

The ads above are what pay for the proxy servers, so the Site checks whether an ad blocker is stopping them. The check runs in your browser only: it looks at whether a test element was hidden and whether our ad containers and the ad networks' scripts loaded. If a blocker is found, your browser keeps a small record in local storage (zofhub.support.v1): how many pages in a row showed a blocker, what kind of evidence it was, a timestamp, and the addresses of up to three ad-network scripts that failed to load. It is never sent to our servers or to an ad network. After three pages with a blocker on, the premium proxy engines (AnuraOS and the standalone browsers) are paused until the blocker is paused; the games and the built-in engines are never affected. The first page you load with the blocker paused clears the record.

To tell a blocked script apart from one that is merely broken, your browser may send a request to that script's address with no cookies and no referrer, which receives nothing that runs. On the proxy browser this only happens when you have told us you paused your blocker and a previous check found network-level blocking; no ad is ever loaded there.

What game recommendations use

To suggest games you might like, we keep a small amount of information in your browser only. It is never stored on our servers, never linked to an account (there are none), and never linked to your IP address.

  • Games you opened — so we can suggest similar ones.
  • Games you starred — a direct "I like this".
  • Games you scrolled past without opening — so the same ones stop coming back.
  • Roughly how long you played — kept only as "under 15 seconds", "under 2 minutes" or "longer", never an exact duration. A game you closed instantly should not count the same as one you played all afternoon.
  • Your last few searches and the last mood you typed into Vibe Finder — the mood is forgotten after 45 minutes.

When you load the homepage or use Vibe Finder, this list is sent with that one request so the server can order the results. The server uses it to build that response and keeps no copy. We do not record mouse movement, scrolling, keystroke timing, or anything about what you do on other sites.

Clearing your browser data — or using the clear-data control in site settings — erases all of it, and recommendations simply go back to showing popular games.

4. Advertising

We do not load Google AdSense or any other Google advertising code — Google's programs are not available to sites like this one, which include a web-proxy feature, so no data is shared with Google for advertising purposes.

Advertising on this Site is served by third-party ad networks. Depending on which are enabled at any given time, these may include:

  • Ad-Maven — a full-screen ad between games, at most once every twenty minutes — privacy policy
  • ExoClick — the header and sidebar banners — privacy policy
  • HilltopAds — an in-page notification-style ad — privacy policy
  • Clickadu — a short video before a game starts, skippable after five seconds — privacy policy
  • Mondiad — a labelled "Sponsored" block below the game recommendations — privacy policy
  • PopAds — a pop-under window, on desktop game pages only — privacy policy

This is the complete list of advertising networks ZoFHub can use. We do not work with any other network, and we never load Google AdSense or Google Ad Manager. At any given time only the networks we have enabled are active — often fewer than the six listed here. None of them is ever loaded on the proxy browser or any other proxy page.

What this means for you:

  • The network — not us — serves the ads, and it may set its own cookies or similar identifiers (including device and approximate location signals) to select, cap and measure them. Any data it collects is governed by that network's privacy policy, not this one. We do not receive, store, or have access to those identifiers.
  • We never send an ad network your display name, your favorites, your scores, or anything you type into the AI assistant or the proxy browser.
  • You can limit interest-based advertising generally at aboutads.info/choices and youronlinechoices.eu, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time. An ad blocker will also stop these networks loading. The games and the built-in proxy engines keep working normally with one enabled; the premium proxy engines are paused while it stays on (see “The ad-block check” in Section 3).

Games embedded from third-party providers may show their own in-game ads. Those run inside the game frame under the provider's own policies — see Section 5.

5. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of reputable providers to run the Site. Your information may be processed by them only as needed to deliver the Site:

  • Hosting — our application host stores server logs and serves the Site.
  • Database — Supabase stores the game catalog, community content, and feedback messages.
  • AI provider — your browser contacts the AI provider directly, from your own device and your own IP address. We do not relay, see, or store your prompts. You choose the provider in AI settings: Pollinations (free, keyless) by default, or optionally Hugging Face (using a free token you store only in your browser) or Puter.js. Puter runs on your own Puter account — it may ask you to sign in the first time, and some of its models spend your own Puter credits (each model's cost is shown). Whichever you pick, the request reaches that provider under their privacy policy, not ours.
  • Game providers — games are embedded from third-party providers, which may set their own cookies inside the game frame under their own privacy policies.
  • Advertising — only the networks listed in Section 4, and only those we have enabled. None of them is ever loaded on the proxy browser or inside a proxied page.

10. International users

We may process and store information in countries other than where you live. By using the Site, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries.

Your approximate country. When the Site is served through a network edge that tells us which country a request comes from (for example Cloudflare's CF-IPCountry header), we use that two-letter country code for that one request only: to send you to the ZoFHub domain for your region, and to choose the ad tags for your region. We do not store or log it. If you would rather stay on the domain you opened, add ?geo=stay to the address; a zh_geo cookie then remembers that choice for seven days.

11. Donations

Donating is entirely optional and buys nothing — no perks, no ad removal, no special access. We run no payment processing of our own. Donations are accepted only in cryptocurrency — there is no card option and no account. The donate dialog does one thing: it shows a receiving address. It has no form, collects no name, email or amount, and sends nothing back to us when you open it or copy an address.

Because of that, we cannot see who donated. Most of the coins offered — Bitcoin, Litecoin, Ethereum, Solana and the USDC stablecoin on Solana — settle on public blockchains, where your transaction is permanently visible to anyone, forever, and donations to the same address can be linked together. That is a property of those networks, not a choice we make. Monero (XMR) is the one private option offered: sender, receiver and amount are hidden on-chain. If an optional swap service is offered to convert another coin, that is a third-party provider operating under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Donations are non-refundable. We will never solicit a payment through chat, a game, or an email — if you are asked to pay in any of those ways, it is not us.
