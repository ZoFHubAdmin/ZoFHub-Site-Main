Last updated: June 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZoFHub ("ZoFHub", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit {{SITE_URL}} (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Leaderboard scores — when you submit a high score, we store the score, the game it belongs to, and the display name your device generated for you.
  • Community posts & recommendations — any text you choose to post.
  • AI prompts — text or image prompts you send to the AI assistant are forwarded to our AI provider to generate a response (see Section 5).

Information collected automatically

  • Device & usage data — browser type, device type, pages viewed, and general interaction data used to keep the Site working and improve it.
  • Server logs — our host records standard log data, which may include your IP address, for security, abuse-prevention, and reliability.
  • Cookies & local storage — small files and browser storage used for preferences, your generated display name, favorites, and advertising. See our Cookie Policy.

We do not ask for your real name, email, password, or payment details to play. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the Site (load games, show leaderboards, run the AI assistant).
  • To remember your preferences, favorites, and display name on your device.
  • To protect the Site against abuse, fraud, and technical problems.
  • To measure and improve performance and content.
  • To display advertising on supported pages.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser local storage for essential functions, to remember your settings, and — on pages that show ads — for advertising. You can control or delete cookies in your browser at any time. For full details and how to opt out, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Advertising

No advertising network is currently active on this Site. We do not load Google AdSense or any other Google advertising code — Google's programs are not available to sites like this one, which include a web-proxy feature, so no data is shared with Google for advertising purposes.

If we enable advertising in future, it will be through a third-party ad network. When that happens:

  • The network — not us — serves the ads, and it may set its own cookies or similar identifiers to show and measure them. Any data it collects is governed by that network's privacy policy, not this one.
  • We will update this section to name the network and link its privacy policy and opt-out before any ads are served.
  • You can limit interest-based advertising generally at aboutads.info/choices and youronlinechoices.eu, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time.

Games embedded from third-party providers may show their own in-game ads. Those run inside the game frame under the provider's own policies — see Section 5.

5. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of reputable providers to run the Site. Your information may be processed by them only as needed to deliver the Site:

  • Hosting — our application host stores server logs and serves the Site.
  • Database — Supabase stores leaderboard scores and community content.
  • AI provider — prompts you send to the AI assistant are forwarded to a third-party AI service to generate responses; we do not attach your identity to them.
  • Game providers — games are embedded from third-party providers, which may set their own cookies inside the game frame under their own privacy policies.
  • Advertising — Google and its partners (see Section 4).

If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Where required, we configure ads to be treated as directed to a general audience and limit personalized advertising accordingly.
