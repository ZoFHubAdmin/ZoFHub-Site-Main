Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how ZoFHub uses cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) on {{SITE_URL}} (the "Site"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Local storage is a similar browser feature that lets a site save small amounts of data on your device. Both let the Site remember information between pages and visits.

2. How we use them

  • Essential — needed for the Site to function (for example, keeping the site secure and remembering your cookie choice). These cannot be switched off.
  • Preferences — remember your generated display name, favorites, recently played games, and settings. These are mostly stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Advertising — we set no advertising cookies of our own. Third-party ad networks may set their own when their ads load. See Section 3.

Advertising frequency cap

Where a full-screen ad is shown between games, we store a single timestamp in local storage (zofhub.adfreq.v1) recording when it last appeared, so it cannot reappear for at least twenty minutes. It holds a time and nothing else — no identifier, no browsing history — it never leaves your browser, and clearing site data removes it. If local storage is unavailable we cannot enforce the cap, and no full-screen ad is shown.

Ad-block check

If an ad blocker is detected, local storage keeps zofhub.support.v1: a count of consecutive pages with a blocker on, the kind of evidence, a timestamp, and up to three ad-network script addresses that failed to load. Session storage keeps zofhub.support.note so the explanatory note is shown once per visit. Neither leaves your browser. See the Privacy Policy, Section 3.

Other functional storage

  • zofhub.inapp.min (session storage) — remembers that you minimised the notice shown inside apps such as TikTok or Instagram.
  • zh_geo (cookie, seven days) — set only if you add ?geo=stay to the address, so you are not sent to your region's ZoFHub domain.

3. Third-party cookies

We do not load Google advertising code (AdSense or otherwise), so no Google advertising cookies are set by this Site.

Cookies you may still encounter come from third parties whose content we embed:

  • Embedded games — games run inside frames from third-party providers, which may set their own cookies under their own policies. This is the main source of third-party cookies on the Site.
  • Ad networks — advertising is served by third-party networks, which may set their own cookies and similar identifiers to select, frequency-cap and measure ads. Those cookies belong to the network, not to us, and we cannot read them. Depending on which are enabled, they may include: Ad-Maven, ExoClick, HilltopAds, Clickadu, Mondiad, and PopAds. Each link goes to that network's own privacy policy and opt-out. That is the complete list — we use no other advertising network, and never Google AdSense.
  • Not on the proxy. No advertising cookie or script of ours is loaded on /browser or any other proxy page. Advertising runs only on ZoFHub's own pages.

4. Your choices

  • Use the cookie banner shown on your first visit to accept or decline non-essential cookies.
  • Clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. Doing so may reset your display name, favorites, and preferences.
  • Limit interest-based advertising generally at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu, or opt out directly with any of the networks listed in Section 3. An ad blocker also stops them loading; the Site is built to work normally with one enabled.

5. Consent

Where required by law, we ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, a consent message is shown before personalized ads are served. You can change your mind at any time by clearing cookies and revisiting the Site.
