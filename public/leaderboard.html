# Player Game Score

About these scores

Scores are reported by players, not read from the games — every game here runs in a sandboxed frame from another site, so there is no way to read a real score out of one. Submissions need a single-use play token, and anything far above the rest of the board is held back for review rather than shown. Treat the board as a friendly scoreboard, not a verified record.
