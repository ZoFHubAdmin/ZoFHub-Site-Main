Before you send anything

Donations are voluntary and non-refundable, and they buy nothing — no perks, no ad removal, no special access. Crypto transactions cannot be reversed: check the address and the network before sending, and send a small test amount first. ZoFHub never asks for a payment through chat, email or a game.

ZoFHub does not run a payment processor and does not watch the blockchain. Nothing here records who sent what — there is no receipt, no donor list and no account to credit. Your wallet is the only record of the transaction.
